## What this site is

Hệ thống lớn retells case studies of large systems from primary engineering sources, in Vietnamese and English. It is a magazine, not documentation: each post tells a story with a beginning and an end. Every post has a top layer you can read in two minutes and a deep layer that takes six to ten.

## The two-layer anatomy

The top layer is the title, a one-line summary, the key numbers, a TL;DR and "For a small team". The deep layer is the story itself: where they started, what broke, the options they weighed, what they built, what it cost, and what went wrong afterwards. The ending is fixed: Lessons (about them, not about you) and Sources.

## Source policy

Primary sources only: the company's own engineering blog, talks by their engineers, and papers. Every number carries a reference written like `[S1 §3]` or `[S2 @12:40]`. A number that cannot be traced is not used. Secondary summaries may inform the reading, but they are never cited.

## How posts are made

Posts are drafted with AI from the primary sources, then reviewed and edited by Ryan against a checklist. Pipeline pull requests never auto-merge. Posts retell in their own words, never reproduce the original, and never use the source's images.

## The selection bar

One great post beats three average ones. Two to three posts a week is a ceiling, not a target. A case study qualifies only with concrete numbers and a real decision, incident, migration or scaling problem behind it.
